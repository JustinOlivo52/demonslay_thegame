# Approved gameplay art direction

Pixel-art slayer, demons and breathing effects, based on the approved mock-combat comparison. Keep a consistent camera, scale, palette, shading and foot anchor. Produce real anticipation, active strike and recovery poses; do not rotate a static full-body portrait as the final animation.

Generated sheets require visual inspection and cleanup. Current run poses are too similar and foot placement varies. Add directional views and in-betweens before production. Water Wheel and finishing sequences need dedicated frames.

The game uses canon Demon Slayer characters and styles as requested, supplemented by original characters and enemies. Detailed illustrated art can remain optional portrait/reference material.

Normal melee: readable character animation, no ground-circle warning. Blood Demon Arts may use area warnings matched to their actual geometry. Audio is deferred; essential signals must remain visible.
