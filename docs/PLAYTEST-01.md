# Combat playtest 01

This checkpoint evaluates movement, sword attacks, dodging and enemy readability. It does not yet evaluate RPG progression.

1. Move horizontally and diagonally. Check that speed feels consistent and arena boundaries hold.
2. Attack from close range, from outside reach, and while aiming away from the demon.
3. Let a warning circle appear. Walk out of it, then try dodging through a strike.
4. Dodge repeatedly until stamina is low. Check whether the stamina cost and recovery feel fair.
5. Defeat the demon. Reset and deliberately lose. Confirm both encounters can restart.
6. Pause with Escape and the button. Switch browser tabs and resume. Check that attacks stop while paused.
7. Resize the browser. Check aiming and readable controls at your usual desktop resolution.

## Feedback to bring back

- What felt good?
- What felt confusing or unfair?
- Movement: too fast, too slow, or comfortable?
- Sword: reach, aiming and timing?
- Dodge: distance, stamina cost and timing?
- Enemy: warning duration, damage and recovery?
- Any bug: what happened, steps to repeat it, browser and screen size.

## Gate

Fix blocking gameplay issues and obtain the player's checkpoint approval before adding breathing forms. Browser playtesting remains necessary even when automated tests pass.
